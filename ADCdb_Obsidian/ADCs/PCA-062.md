Related Clinical Trial NCT Number NCT02375958 Clinical Status Phase 1 Clinical Description A phase 1 multi-center, open-label dose escalation and expansion study of PCA062 administered intravenously in adult patients with p-CAD positive tumors. Primary Endpoint The MTD was PCA062 3.60 mg/kg every 2 weeks.No patient achieved a complete response. Only 1 patient with stage IV metastatic HNSCC treated at 0.90 mg/kg achieved a confirmed partial response (PR) as best overall response (BOR). The disease control rate (DCR) for the 31 patients with other tumors was 22.60% (95% CI, 9.60-41.10). In patients with HNSCC (n = 6), DCR was 33.30% (95% CI, 4.30-77.70), and in patients with esophageal cancer (n = 9), DCR was 22.20% (95% CI, 2.80-60.00).
