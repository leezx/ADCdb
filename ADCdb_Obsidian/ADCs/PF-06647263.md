Primary Endpoint Six (10.00%) patients achieved a confirmed partial response and 22 (36.70%) patients had stable disease. No correlations were observed between tumor responses and EFNA4 expression levels. Study findings showed manageable safety and favorable PK for PF-06647263 administered QW at the RP2D,with preliminary evidence of limited antitumor activity in patients with TNBC and ovarian cancer. Other Endpoint The RP2D was determined to be 0.015 mg/kg QW.
