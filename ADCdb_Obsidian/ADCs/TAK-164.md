Related Clinical Trial NCT Number NCT03449030 Clinical Status Phase 1 Clinical Description An open-label, dose escalation, phase 1, first-in-human study of TAK-164, an antibody-drug conjugate, in patients with advanced gastrointestinal cancers expressing guanylyl cyclase C. Primary Endpoint Dosing was capped at 0.19 mg/kg due to hepatic toxicity. The RP2D was determined as 0.064 mg/kg but was considered insufficient to derive significant clinical benefit. Other Endpoint No pts had dose-limiting toxicities (DLT) in cycle 1 up to 0.32 mg/kg. TAK-164 appeared to have a manageable safety profile up to 0.064 mg/kg in pts with advanced GI cancers.
