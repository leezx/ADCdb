Related Clinical Trial NCT Number NCT02092792 Clinical Status Phase 1 Clinical Description A phase 1, open-label study evaluating the safety and tolerability of escalating doses of DLYE5953A in patients with refractory solid tumors. Primary Endpoint The confirmed overall objective response rate was 17.76%. All responders (8/68 patients) had PR,and all had received the RP2D dose of 2.40 mg/kg. This included three patients with MBC, and five patients in the NSCLC expansion cohort (5/25; 20%). Other Endpoint The recommended phase II dose (RP2D) was 2.40 mg/kg Q3W. No dose-limiting toxicities were identified during dose escalation (0.20-2.40 mg/kg; n = 20).
