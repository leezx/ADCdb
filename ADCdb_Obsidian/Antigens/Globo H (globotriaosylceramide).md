| Function     | Globo H (globotriaosylceramide) is a global-series glycosphingolipid antigen that is present on the outer membrane of some cancer cells. Globo H is not expressed in normal tissue cells but is expressed in a number of types of cancers, including cancers of the breast, prostate, and pancreas. Globo H's exclusivity for cancer cells makes it a target of interest for cancer therapies. OBI-888 OBI-999 Monomethyl auristatin E Microtubule (MT) |
