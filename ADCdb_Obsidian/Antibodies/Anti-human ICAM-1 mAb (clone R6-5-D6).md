## Related ADC Activity Data

### ICAM-1-IR700 (Investigative)

**Source:** Discovered Using Cell Line-derived Xenograft Model
**Experiment 1 Reporting the Activity Date of This ADC**

- **Tumor Growth Inhibition value (TGI):** ≈ 63.22% (Day 28) High ICAM-1 expression ( ICAM-1+++ )
- **Method Description:** Female homozygote athymic nude mice, 6-8 weeks old, MDA-MB-468-luc (4x10 6 ) or MDA-MB-231 (1x10 6 ) cells were inoculated into the right dorsum of mice. ICAM-1-IR700 (100 g) was injected 9 and 25days after cell inoculation, respectively (day 1). The mice were killed with CO 2 when the tumor volume reached 2000 mm 3 .
- **In Vitro Model:** Breast adenocarcinoma MDA-MB-231 cells CVCL_0062
